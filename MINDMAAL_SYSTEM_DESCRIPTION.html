1. What MindMaal is

MindMaal (MM) is not a new engine -- it is a grading layer built on top of the same core mechanics already proven in Dhagax Dailies: daily trend and bias, point-of-interest (POI) zones (order blocks, fair value gaps, rejection blocks), the session liquidity sweep rule, the premium/discount 50% gate, and the 5-minute stop-entry trigger. Dhagax Dailies treated a trade as pass/fail -- it either qualified under CONTROL or it didn't. MindMaal keeps every one of those building blocks and adds a classification layer on top: every real trade is graded into one of eight tiers based on three independent properties --

  • Trend alignment -- is the trade with the daily trend, or against it?
  • Timing -- is it taken on the day price actually impacts the zone, or on a later continuation day?
  • Setup timeframe -- is the reacting POI on the 1-hour chart, or the 4-hour chart?

These three properties combine into eight grades: A+, A, B+, B, AB, BA, AB+, BA+. For now, every grade is risked identically -- grading is a classification and analysis layer only. Differentiated position sizing per grade is a future decision, made only once backtest data earns it (see Section 13).

2. Shared mechanics (apply to every grade)

These five mechanics are identical across all eight grades. They are defined once here rather than repeated in every grade's own section.

2.1 Session liquidity sweep

No entry can trigger in London or New York until the prior session's own liquidity has already been taken:

  • Trading in London requires the Asian session's high (for a sell) or low (for a buy) to already be swept.
  • Trading in New York requires London's own high/low to already be swept -- unless the Asian sweep already happened at any point before London's own close, in which case that one sweep covers both sessions.

In the grade specs below this appears as "AL/LL taken" (Asian Low / London Low, for a buy) or "AH/LH taken" (for a sell).

2.2 Premium/discount 50% gate

Before any entry search begins, price must reach the midpoint between the setup zone's protect level and the last opposing confirmed swing -- a buy reacts from the discount half, a sell from the premium half. This check applies to the setup-timeframe POI in every grade, whether or not the grade requires the trade to happen on the daily impact day.

2.3 5-minute stop-entry trigger

Once the setup POI has reacted and premium/discount is satisfied, a 5-minute swing high (for a sell) or swing low (for a buy) candidate is marked as the stop-entry trigger level. The trade is only live once price exceeds that level and the order fills.

2.4 Session timing window

Entry can only trigger in the first two hours of London or New York:

SessionSummer (DST)Winter
London10:00-12:00 Riyadh11:00-13:00 Riyadh
New York15:00-17:00 Riyadh16:00-18:00 Riyadh

2.5 Structural invalidation

A zone dies the moment a candle's body closes through it, or its anchor level breaks -- at any point before entry, not only at formation. A dead zone cannot be traded regardless of which grade it would otherwise qualify for.

2.6 SL placement (mandatory, carried from Dhagax Dailies item 1)

SL always sits behind the raw 1-minute price extreme from the zone's real impact_time through the entry minute -- never a confirmed 5-minute swing pivot, and never clamped to the trading-window open. This must be the only SL logic in MM's code, for every grade.

2.7 Pre-session premium/discount marking (mandatory, carried from Dhagax Dailies items 9/10/11b)

If a daily POI is impacted before the trading session opens, and the pre-session move is bigger than 15 pips, standard same-session premium/discount does not apply. Instead:

  • Mark the extreme price reached pre-session as the premium (sell) / discount (buy) boundary.
  • Wait for price to return into that marked area before searching for entry.
  • If price keeps moving further away before returning, the mark keeps extending to the new extreme -- it is never fixed at the first pre-session reading.
  • While waiting, watch for two separate invalidation checks running in parallel: the ordinary anchor break (Section 2.5), and a distinct swing-confirmation violation -- if price takes out the previous 1H candle's low/high (mirrored for a 4H setup) before entry, the setup is discredited outright, not just noted.

Below the 15-pip threshold, this mechanism does not engage -- treat the impact as an ordinary same-session reaction.

2.8 Aggressive-POI eligibility (mandatory, carried from Dhagax Dailies item 14)

An aggressive RB and an aggressive OB use identical eligibility logic -- same trigger conditions, same impact rules, on both the 1H and 4H setup timeframes. There is no separate rule for one zone type over the other.

2.9 Backtest window padding (mandatory, carried from Dhagax Dailies item 22)

Every MM backtest run extends its data window past the period's own end date so trailing open trades can actually resolve, instead of being reported as "still open" purely because the run cut off too early.

3. The grading logic

Three properties, each binary, determine a grade:

PropertyOption AOption B
Trend alignmentWith-trend (in-favor POI)Counter-trend (opposing POI)
TimingImpact day onlyContinuation day (price action, not impact)
Setup timeframe1-hour4-hour

A+ and A are the 1-hour, impact-day grades (with-trend and counter-trend respectively). B+ and B are their 4-hour, continuation-day counterparts. AB and BA are the 4-hour versions of A+ and A -- either the impact day itself on 4H, or a next-day continuation confirmed by a wick close rather than a body close. AB+ and BA+ are not separately designed grades -- they are the natural overlap case where a single 4H trade satisfies both its B-side condition (body close) and its A-side condition (wick close) on the same day, discovered while working through the spec rather than invented in advance.

4. Grade A+

A+

with-trend1H setupimpact day only

The cleanest grade: trend, timeframe, and timing all aligned in the trade's favor.

  • Trend: daily market must be trending; we only trade with it (buy in an uptrend, sell in a downtrend).
  • Daily POI: any in-favor POI type -- OB, FVG, or RB -- impacted today (the impact day itself, no later).
  • Daily liquidity: PDL (buy) or PDH (sell) must be taken.
  • Structure confirmation: either a 1H MSS shift into the trade's direction (price came into the buying POI from a 1H downtrend and shifted up), or an aggressive 1H POI formed on the last pullback leg of the opposing 1H trend.
  • Premium/discount: price must react to the 1H POI from the discount (buy) or premium (sell) half.
  • Entry: 5m stop-entry trigger, fired in the session's first two hours only.
  • Permitted: the daily POI can be any of OB/FVG/RB as long as in-favor. The 1H POI can be in-favor or aggressive. An impact candle opening inside the 1H POI is allowed -- but must be flagged in the backtest output, not treated as a disqualifying bug.

5. Grade A

A

counter-trend1H setupimpact day or later

Everything A+ requires, except the daily POI is opposing (counter-trend) and the trade doesn't have to happen on the impact day itself.

  • Trend: daily market trending one way; we trade against it, from the opposing POI (aggressive or already-formed).
  • Timing: impact day, or any later day, as long as the daily POI has not been structurally violated (Section 2.5) and PDL/PDH is still taken on the actual day of the trade.
  • All other mechanics (1H structure confirmation, premium/discount, entry, impact-inside-zone allowance) are identical to A+.

6. Grade B+

B+

with-trend4H setupcontinuation day

Trades trend continuation without requiring the daily impact day or a daily POI reaction at all.

  • Trend: with-trend only.
  • Continuation day: yesterday's candle body closed beyond the day before yesterday's candle, in the trend's direction. This is the day's qualifying condition -- it replaces the impact-day/PDL requirement entirely.
  • Setup: a 4H POI, in-favor or aggressive (same logic as 1H's structure confirmation, one timeframe up).
  • Premium/discount: still required on the 4H POI, just not tied to a specific impact day -- in a strong trend, price can run for days before the check is satisfied.
  • Session liquidity, timing window, and 5m entry mechanics unchanged from A+/A.
  • Impact candle opening inside the 4H POI is allowed, and must be recorded.

7. Grade B

B

counter-trend4H setupcontinuation (reversal) day

B+'s mirror: a reversal signal against the prevailing daily trend.

  • Continuation day: yesterday's body closed against the overall daily trend relative to the day before -- e.g. market is up, but yesterday closed below the day before yesterday.
  • Setup: 4H opposing POI, selling into an uptrend or buying into a downtrend.
  • All other mechanics identical to B+.

8. Grade AB

AB

with-trend4H setuptwo paths

The 4H version of A+. Reached by either of two paths.

  • Path 1 -- same-day: A+'s full criteria, but the setup is 4H instead of 1H.
  • Path 2 -- next-day continuation: an A+ zone existed yesterday (whether or not it was actually traded), survived the day structurally alive, and yesterday's candle wick (not necessarily the body) closed beyond the day before yesterday's candle. Today, that same direction is traded with a 4H setup.

9. Grade BA

BA

counter-trend4H setuptwo paths

The 4H version of A -- same relationship AB has to A+.

  • Path 1 -- same-day: A's full criteria (counter-trend, opposing POI, PDL/PDH taken today), 4H setup instead of 1H.
  • Path 2 -- next-day continuation: an A zone from yesterday survived, with a wick-close beyond the day before -- traded today with a 4H setup.

10. Grade AB+

AB+

with-trend4H setupoverlap

The case where B+ and AB are both true on the same day for the same trade.

  • Yesterday's candle body closed beyond the day before (B+'s own trigger).
  • And a surviving A+ zone from yesterday also had its wick close beyond the day before (AB's own trigger) -- since a body-close-beyond always implies the wick reached at least as far, this overlap happens whenever an A+ zone happens to exist on a B+-qualifying day.
  • Traded today with a 4H setup, same mechanics as both parent grades.

11. Grade BA+

BA+

counter-trend4H setupoverlap

BA+'s counter-trend mirror: B and BA both true on the same day.

  • Yesterday's body closed against the overall daily trend (B's trigger).
  • And a surviving A zone from yesterday also had its wick close beyond the day before (BA's trigger).
  • Traded today with a 4H setup.

13. Risk policy

Every grade is risked identically for now. No grade gets a larger or smaller position size, and no grade is prioritized over another in execution. Differentiated risk per grade is only introduced later, and only once enough backtest data across all eight grades shows a real, evidenced performance difference that justifies it -- not before.

14. What must be recorded

The backtest output must flag, for every trade, whether the entry's impact candle opened inside its setup-timeframe POI (1H for A+/A, 4H for everything else). This is a permitted condition, not a bug or a disqualification -- it must simply be visible in the output so it can be reviewed later.

Two further fields are mandatory in every MM trade row, carried from Dhagax Dailies item 25, with no exceptions:

  • MFE ≤ 1 pip flag -- any trade where price never moved more than 1 pip in our favor before resolving.
  • SL overshoot ≤ 1 pip flag -- any losing trade where price exceeded the SL level by 1 pip or less.

Both must exist as real columns from MM's very first run, not added retroactively.

15. Data handling (mandatory, carried from Dhagax Dailies item 4)

Raw price data must not be stripped at the week-open/week-close boundary in a way that discards real trading. The week closes exactly where the Daily engine's own NY-anchored "Friday" candle closes (01:00 Riyadh Saturday winter / 00:00 summer) -- real tick data genuinely continues into that window and must be kept, not blanket-deleted the way a naive weekend filter would.

18. Candidate rule under review -- not yet confirmed standing

Proactive opposing-POI violation (as a B+/B trigger). Spotted 2026-10-13 while walking Jan 5-7, 2026 by hand. Not yet merged into Section 5/6's B+/B definition -- recorded here until seen on more days.

  • The situation: an opposing-type daily POI (an A/A-style zone, holding a pullback leg against the prevailing trend) gets its body closed into by the very next daily candle -- the candle's close lands inside the opposing zone's own range, without necessarily closing all the way beyond it (the plain body-close-beyond-range check in continuation_day(), Section 6's current test, can come back empty here).
  • The read: that close-into-the-zone is itself treated as confirming the pullback failed to hold -- a proactive violation, named by analogy to Dhagax Dailies' own proactive-flip CONTROL mechanism (a POI failing to hold triggers an assumption even without full structural confirmation). No A+/A/AB/AB+ setup is read off that opposing POI once this happens.
  • The effect: the day's setup reverts to trend continuation -- B+ if the resulting direction is with-trend, B if counter. Example: 2026-01-05's RB (bullish, opposing a down-trend, holding the pullback leg) had 2026-01-06's body close inside it (1.16882, inside the RB's 1.16587-1.17201 range) -- read as a proactive violation, giving 2026-01-07 a B+ (SELL) setup instead of the A (BUY) the same-day Daily POI impact would otherwise have implied.
  • Open question: exact threshold (does ANY close-inside count, or only close-inside-past-some-fraction-of-the-zone?) and whether it ever produces AB+/BA+-style overlaps with the plain continuation check. Needs more examples before being written into Section 6 as a confirmed rule.

19. Dual-break-candle anchor correction -- a reusable pattern, applied per-case

Confirmed 2026-10-14 via RB#416, candle 16 on 2026-01-02 (a 1H BUY zone whose protect level was wrongly set to candle 16's own low instead of the real, older swing low at candle 13:00, 1.17128). Three attempts to fix this inside the shared swing engine (weekly_combined_generator.py) all corrupted OTHER zones on OTHER days -- that engine's swing tracking is sequential, so any change to it ripples forward through every zone ever formed. All three were reverted; the shared engine is untouched. This section documents the correct mental model and an isolated, per-zone replay function (resolve_dual_break_anchor in all_tf_combined_generator.py) that implements it without touching the shared engine at all -- applied to a specific zone when this exact pattern is diagnosed there, not wired in automatically.

  • The problem: a "dual-break" candle breaks BOTH its prior candle's high AND low. If price breaks the prior low first (in real clock time) and only later breaks the prior high, the shared engine can end up crediting the dual-break candle's own low as the swing low that the second break's confirmation. A swing anchor should always come from an earlier, separate candle -- a candle cannot validly serve as both the proof and the subject of its own pivot.
  • The live-trading model (not a look-ahead; this is how it must work moment-to-moment, since a trader can't know in advance how the candle will close):
    1. The candle's first break of the prior low is live and real: it confirms the prior candle's high as an official swing high (already correct, unaffected), and separately marks the candle's own low-so-far as a provisional anchor. Trade search runs against this provisional anchor immediately -- never withheld pending the candle's close.
    2. If price later, still within the SAME candle, breaks back below that provisional anchor, the provisional anchor is retired and this moment becomes a fresh, second impact. Trade search resumes against the new, lower running anchor from here.
    3. Once the candle actually closes: a BEARISH close means the candle's real story was "up first, then down" -- there was never a genuine swing low on this candle at all, and the TRUE anchor reverts to whatever the real, older swing low was before this candle. A BULLISH close means the anchor stands, final value = the candle's own lowest point reached. (Mirrored for a SELL/high-anchor zone: bearish close keeps the candle's own anchor, bullish close reverts to the true prior one.)
  • Worked example (RB#416, candle 16, 2026-01-02, bearish close): first impact at 16:10 (provisional anchor 1.17199) -- blocked from entry by premium/discount (1.17199 sits in the zone's premium half, 1.17128-1.17207 range, midline 1.171675). Second impact at 16:30 when price broke back below 1.17199 -- blocked by no session sweep (NY requires London's own session low swept first; it never was). Candle 16 closed bearish, so the true, final anchor reverts to 1.17128 (candle 13:00's real swing low, confirmed 15:02) -- matching what the isolated fix now computes. Net result: no trade either way, but for the correct reason.
  • If this pattern is seen again (a zone's protect level or impact time looks wrong on a dual-break candle), raise it specifically and apply resolve_dual_break_anchor to that zone -- do not attempt another shared-engine fix without first proving (across the full dataset, not just the day in question) that it changes nothing else.
