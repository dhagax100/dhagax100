in the folder — nothing gets renamed or duplicated. New days go at the
TOP, newest first. This is the JOURNAL (what happened, day by day) —
not the rule book. Rules/code changes live in DAILIES_LEARNING_LOG.txt.

Trade taken / Result / Notes. Short, plain, no fluff.

SYSTEM STATUS — this day exposed two more real bugs, both fixed and
verified before this entry was written:
- find_prev_day_extreme() had a real off-by-one: it looked up
  "yesterday" against the Daily engine's own bars, which are indexed to
  the 17:00-NY close (01:00 Riyadh in winter) — one hour after real
  midnight — so a midnight probe always landed on the day BEFORE the
  one intended. Fixed to compute directly from real M1 data over the
  actual previous calendar day.
- That fix immediately exposed a second, more serious gap: with the
  day-lookup now correct, 12 Jan itself would have had 1H abandoned
  from 01:08 Riyadh onward (11 Jan's low got swept that early) — which
  would have silently erased the RB#67/72 trade, the very first
  verified result of this whole project. The react-day rule only
  applies on an actual react day (the day AFTER a Daily POI's own
  impact) — never on the impact day itself, which 12 Jan is. Fixed with
  a same-day-impact check that skips the sweep rule entirely on an
  impact day. 12 Jan's trade is restored and unchanged; 13 Jan's
  no-trade conclusion is unaffected either way.
- Separately noted, not yet acted on: the premium/discount gate used
  throughout this project turns out to be a NEW rule (user's own
  words) — not part of the validated five_bso_engine.py port or
  SPEC.md's actual H4/5m entry process. Tabled deliberately until it's
  a real bottleneck with more data to check it against.

- RB#69 (BUY, 17:25) and FVG#9/FVG#31 (BUY) — wrong side for the day's
  bias, and FVG#9/FVG#31 also never reached premium/discount on their

14:55 — 23 minutes after entry, well before the eventual SL at 16:08.
Real BE-research data point: if a future rule moves SL to BE once price
reaches that pre-impact swing, this trade would have been saved.

LESSON: most POIs in a leg die fast once a fresher one takes over —
impact alone isn't confirmation. A POI whose SL and invalidation level
coincide is fragile by construction; don't treat "impacted" as strong
confirmation on its own.
