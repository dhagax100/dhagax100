DHAGAX DAILIES — TRADING JOURNAL
==================================
One file. Same name every time. Whatever you save over this replaces it
in the folder — nothing gets renamed or duplicated. New days go at the
TOP, newest first. This is the JOURNAL (what happened, day by day) —
not the rule book. Rules/code changes live in DAILIES_LEARNING_LOG.txt.

Entry format, every day: Bias / Session / Daily driver / 1H+4H context /
Trade taken / Result / Notes. Short, plain, no fluff.


2026-01-12 (Monday) — SELL day
---------------------------------
BIAS: SELL. Session: winter hours, 11:00-21:00 Riyadh.

DAILY DRIVER: FVG#1 (SELL), impacted 10:26 Riyadh — this is the zone
that set the day's bias, before the trading window even opened.

1H CONTEXT: Three SELL zones impacted in one unbroken leg under FVG#1 —
OB#44 (14:00), RB#67 (14:13), RB#68 (16:07). Per the same-leg rule,
only one gets to search for a fresh entry at a time: OB#44 handed off
to RB#67 at 14:13 (RB#67 impacted while OB#44 was still alive), and
RB#67 handed off to RB#68 at 16:07.

TRADE TAKEN: SELL, entry 1.16847 @ 14:32 (from RB#67, before it was
superseded), SL 1.16892 @ 16:08, TP 1.16712 (never hit).
RESULT: SL hit. -1R (4.5 pips risked).
MFE 9.4 pips / MAE 5.9 pips.

WHY IT DIED: RB#67's own protect_level (1.16892) broke at 16:08 — same
minute as the SL. Not a coincidence to chase: the SL and the zone's own
invalidation were the same level here, so the loss and the zone dying
happened together. RB#68 (the next zone in the leg) impacted one minute
earlier (16:07) and was itself breached almost immediately (16:08) —
never got a chance to search for its own entry.

WHAT DIDN'T TRADE, AND WHY:
- RB#69 (BUY, 17:25) and FVG#9/FVG#31 (BUY) — wrong side for the day's
  bias, and FVG#9/FVG#31 also never reached premium/discount on their
  own terms regardless.
- OB#45 (BUY) — never impacted at all.

STRUCTURAL NOTE LOGGED: price reached the pre-impact swing (1.16754) at
14:55 — 23 minutes after entry, well before the eventual SL at 16:08.
Real BE-research data point: if a future rule moves SL to BE once price
reaches that pre-impact swing, this trade would have been saved.

LESSON: most POIs in a leg die fast once a fresher one takes over —
impact alone isn't confirmation. A POI whose SL and invalidation level
coincide is fragile by construction; don't treat "impacted" as strong
confirmation on its own.
