2026-01-14 (Wednesday) — SELL day, "react day," no trade
---------------------------------
BIAS: SELL (unchanged). FVG#1 (Daily) still the controlling parent,
still alive — 14 Jan's own Daily engine had zero events of its own.

REACT-DAY CHECK: is 14 Jan even a react day? Yes — no Daily SELL POI
impacted ON 14 Jan itself (bias is entirely carried in), so the
prior-day-extreme-sweep rule applies. 13 Jan's low (1.16339) was NOT
taken by 14 Jan — both 1H and 4H stayed in play all day.

4H CONTEXT: zero SELL POIs impacted at all on 14 Jan.

1H CONTEXT: four SELL POIs impacted — OB#59/RB#94 (same zone pair,
15:57), RB#88 (05:51), RB#92 (12:15), RB#96 (18:58). All either
structurally breached within a minute of impact, or found no entry
before the window closed. One FVG never reached its own
premium/discount midpoint.

TRADE TAKEN: none.
RESULT: no trade, flat day.

SYSTEM STATUS — this day exposed two more real bugs, both fixed and
verified before this entry was written:
- find_prev_day_extreme() had a real off-by-one: it looked up
  "yesterday" against the Daily engine's own bars, which are indexed to
  the 17:00-NY close (01:00 Riyadh in winter) — one hour after real
  midnight — so a midnight probe always landed on the day BEFORE the
  one intended. Fixed to compute directly from real M1 data over the
  actual previous calendar day.
- That fix immediately exposed a second, more serious gap: with the
  day-lookup now correct, 12 Jan itself would have had 1H abandoned
  from 01:08 Riyadh onward (11 Jan's low got swept that early) — which
  would have silently erased the RB#67/72 trade, the very first
  verified result of this whole project. The react-day rule only
  applies on an actual react day (the day AFTER a Daily POI's own
  impact) — never on the impact day itself, which 12 Jan is. Fixed with
  a same-day-impact check that skips the sweep rule entirely on an
  impact day. 12 Jan's trade is restored and unchanged; 13 Jan's
  no-trade conclusion is unaffected either way.
- Separately noted, not yet acted on: the premium/discount gate used
  throughout this project turns out to be a NEW rule (user's own
  words) — not part of the validated five_bso_engine.py port or
  SPEC.md's actual H4/5m entry process. Tabled deliberately until it's
  a real bottleneck with more data to check it against.

- Cross-day Daily-parent-POI visibility: a Daily POI (FVG#1) whose only

- high_first() dual-break ordering: was comparing this candle's own

  real Daily bars/M1 data every run, no more manual verification

2026-01-12 (Monday) — SELL day
---------------------------------
BIAS: SELL. Session: winter hours, 11:00-21:00 Riyadh.

DAILY DRIVER: FVG#1 (SELL), impacted 10:26 Riyadh — this is the zone
that set the day's bias, before the trading window even opened.

1H CONTEXT: Three SELL zones impacted in one unbroken leg under FVG#1 —
OB#44 (14:00), RB#67 (14:13), RB#68 (16:07). Per the same-leg rule,
only one gets to search for a fresh entry at a time: OB#44 handed off
to RB#67 at 14:13 (RB#67 impacted while OB#44 was still alive), and
RB#67 handed off to RB#68 at 16:07.

TRADE TAKEN: SELL, entry 1.16847 @ 14:32 (from RB#67, before it was
superseded), SL 1.16892 @ 16:08, TP 1.16712 (never hit).
RESULT: SL hit. -1R (4.5 pips risked).
MFE 9.4 pips / MAE 5.9 pips.

WHY IT DIED: RB#67's own protect_level (1.16892) broke at 16:08 — same
minute as the SL. Not a coincidence to chase: the SL and the zone's own
invalidation were the same level here, so the loss and the zone dying
happened together. RB#68 (the next zone in the leg) impacted one minute
earlier (16:07) and was itself breached almost immediately (16:08) —
never got a chance to search for its own entry.

WHAT DIDN'T TRADE, AND WHY:
- RB#69 (BUY, 17:25) and FVG#9/FVG#31 (BUY) — wrong side for the day's
  bias, and FVG#9/FVG#31 also never reached premium/discount on their
  own terms regardless.
- OB#45 (BUY) — never impacted at all.

STRUCTURAL NOTE LOGGED: price reached the pre-impact swing (1.16754) at
14:55 — 23 minutes after entry, well before the eventual SL at 16:08.
Real BE-research data point: if a future rule moves SL to BE once price
reaches that pre-impact swing, this trade would have been saved.

LESSON: most POIs in a leg die fast once a fresher one takes over —
impact alone isn't confirmation. A POI whose SL and invalidation level
coincide is fragile by construction; don't treat "impacted" as strong
confirmation on its own.
